# Hello, I'm Will Balmer.

I am an astronomer, science journalist, and author. I use big telescopes on the ground and in space to study planets in distant solar systems. My research focuses on directly imaging exoplanets using coronagraphy and optical interferometry, to learn more about how planets form and place our own solar system in a galatic context. I am a currently a prize postdoctoral fellow at Northwestern University. I earned my Ph.D in Astrophysics from Johns Hopkins University, where I did my dissertation research at the Space Telescope Science Institute with Dr. Laurent Pueyo. I am incredibly passionate about astronomy outreach, science writing, and mentorship. I am always happy to answer your questions about astronomy research, exoplanets, and telescopes. You can find my CV, links to my first author papers, and some of my talks and articles on this webpage. Clear skies!
